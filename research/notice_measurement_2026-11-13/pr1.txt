The Procurement Regulations 2024 Part 1 Introductory Citation and commencement 1 1 These Regulations may be cited as the Procurement Regulations 2024. 2 These Regulations, except regulation 48 , come into force at the same time at which section 11 of the Procurement Act 2023 comes into force for any purpose . 3 Regulation 48 comes into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made. Section 11 comes into force on such day as a Minister of the Crown may by regulations appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes. See section 127 of the Procurement Act 2023.
