Application of Parts 2 and 3: devolved Welsh and Scottish authorities
Application of Parts 2 and 3 in relation to devolved Scottish authorities
50
Parts 2 and 3 apply to a contracting authority that is a devolved Scottish authority in relation to a procurement which is regulated in accordance with section 115A of, and   Schedule 9A   to, the PA 2023.
Reg. 50  in force at 24.2.2025, see  reg. 1(2)
