## Sources and what they establish

[Source receipts](../research/section53_2026-11-13/law-sources.json) hash the
primary legislation.gov.uk XML with retrieval instants and provision locators.
Exact originals remain at recorded local paths outside Git; committed text is
a derived reading aid, not a replacement for the original. The table in the
light-touch schedule is included separately in that extract.

- PA section 53(1): a public-contract details notice is due before the end of
  30 days beginning with entry, or 120 days for a light-touch contract. Entry
  is day one. Section 53(3)'s 90/180-day contract-copy deadlines are a different
  duty and are not implemented by this rule.
- Section 53(6) excludes private utilities and section 41/Schedule 5 paragraph
  15 user-choice awards. Section 53(4)'s Welsh/Northern Irish exception applies
  to contract copies under (3), not the notice duty under (1).
- Sections 2-3 and Schedule 2 govern contracting authority/public contract
  scope and exemptions. Section 94 permits withholding in specified
  circumstances, sometimes without a public withholding notice. Missing
  publication alone cannot establish a break.
- Section 9, regulation 42 and Schedule 1 define light-touch services. A missing
  light-touch field is not evidence that a contract is ordinary. Regulation 43
  disapplies the Act to regulated health procurement.
- Regulations 32-35 prescribe notice content for competitive awards, framework
  establishment, framework call-offs and direct awards. Regulation 32(2)(l)
  requires the date of contract entry, incorporated by the other three routes.
- Regulation 5 addresses platform publication, successful-submission
  confirmation and qualifying alternative systems. Later public availability
  cannot exclude an earlier qualifying event. Effective versions matter:
  the platform-unavailable alternative wording took effect March 24, 2026.
- Regulation 49 requires separate treatment of devolved Welsh arrangements;
  regulation 50 covers specified Scottish procurement. The earlier candidate
  107781-2026 carries Welsh-regulation classifications. It is not automatically
  an English regulation-32 example. No territorial inference is made from a
  supplier or buyer name.
- Commencement is February 24, 2025, with saved procurements under SI 2024/716
  regulation 5. A later signature date does not itself establish the new regime.
- Section 87(3) uses a different, reasonably-practicable duty for notifiable
  below-threshold contracts; a UK7 marker alone does not select section 53.

The [Cabinet Office guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-contract-details-notices-html),
paragraphs 23-24, says contract entry is not defined in the Act or regulations
and is fact-specific; signature may coincide with entry but need not. The
platform's UK7 description instead speaks of signature. Those are different
statements, not a verified field-to-law equivalence. Paragraph 22 of the
Cabinet Office guidance also says 30 or 90 days, inconsistent with section
53(1)'s 30/120. The enacted text controls; the apparent guidance error is not
encoded. [Guidance receipts](../research/section53_2026-11-13/guidance-sources.json).

## Next proof and current gate

The adapter must preserve date precision, source pointers, missing fields,
separate contract/notice identities and refusal receipts. The rule must use
cited entry evidence, regime and scope; it must not silently relabel a
signature date as entry, a release revision as initial publication, or a
parent notice as the relevant contract-details event. HOLDS and BREAKS need
the same sourced terms and margin. A displayed late notice is insufficient
for BREAKS when a prior acknowledgement or lawful alternative is unresolved.

The owner was asked about the signature/entry distinction under
COLLABORATION section 6, which reserves legal questions to the owner. Pending
that answer, no legal equivalence is assumed. Adapter protection and exact
calendar arithmetic can be completed independently. PR #18 remains draft;
its invariant core is an explicit unmerged dependency, not a main-branch fact.
