## Sources and what they establish

[Source receipts](../research/section53_2026-11-13/law-sources.json) hash the
primary legislation.gov.uk XML with retrieval instants and provision locators.
Exact originals remain at recorded local paths outside Git; committed text is
a derived reading aid, not a replacement for the original. The table in the
light-touch schedule is included separately in that extract.

- PA section 53(1): a public-contract details notice is due before the end of
  30 days beginning with entry, or 120 days for a light-touch contract. Entry
  is day one. Section 53(3)'s 90/180-day contract-copy deadlines are a different
  duty and are not implemented by this rule.
- Section 53(6) excludes private utilities and section 41/Schedule 5 paragraph
  15 user-choice awards. Section 53(4)'s Welsh/Northern Irish exception applies
  to contract copies under (3), not the notice duty under (1).
- Sections 2-3 and Schedule 2 govern contracting authority/public contract
  scope and exemptions. Section 94 permits withholding in specified
  circumstances, sometimes without a public withholding notice. Missing
  publication alone cannot establish a break.
- Section 9, regulation 42 and Schedule 1 define light-touch services. A missing
  light-touch field is not evidence that a contract is ordinary. Regulation 43
  disapplies the Act to regulated health procurement.
- Regulations 32-35 prescribe notice content for competitive awards, framework
  establishment, framework call-offs and direct awards. Regulation 32(2)(l)
  requires the date of contract entry, incorporated by the other three routes.
- Regulation 5 addresses platform publication, successful-submission
  confirmation and qualifying alternative systems. Later public availability
  cannot exclude an earlier qualifying event. Effective versions matter:
  the platform-unavailable alternative wording took effect March 24, 2026.
- Regulation 49 requires separate treatment of devolved Welsh arrangements;
  regulation 50 covers specified Scottish procurement. The earlier candidate
  107781-2026 carries Welsh-regulation classifications. It is not automatically
  an English regulation-32 example. No territorial inference is made from a
  supplier or buyer name.
- Commencement is February 24, 2025, with saved procurements under SI 2024/716
  regulation 5. A later signature date does not itself establish the new regime.
- Section 87(3) uses a different, reasonably-practicable duty for notifiable
  below-threshold contracts; a UK7 marker alone does not select section 53.

The [Cabinet Office guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-contract-details-notices-html),
paragraphs 23-24, says contract entry is not defined in the Act or regulations
and is fact-specific; signature may coincide with entry but need not. The
platform's UK7 description instead speaks of signature. Those are different
statements, not a verified field-to-law equivalence. Paragraph 22 of the
Cabinet Office guidance also says 30 or 90 days, inconsistent with section
53(1)'s 30/120. The enacted text controls; the apparent guidance error is not
encoded. [Guidance receipts](../research/section53_2026-11-13/guidance-sources.json).

## Implemented design and owner decision

The owner approved the conservative scope on November 13: a cited source must
explicitly confirm contract entry. The adapter preserves `dateSigned` as a
signature field and never maps it to entry. The rule's typed entry event rejects
a signature event even when it has a citation. Entry, publication, reviewed
scope, profile thresholds and day-count convention have separate provenance.
Exact integer calendar arithmetic feeds the existing Decimal invariant core;
no core source changed. Profile data carries the 30/120-day limits and the
entry-day-one convention with section-specific hashes and locators.

The adapter uses the watch's existing projection, admission, frozen-capture and
replay interfaces. It retains only identifiers, legal-basis codes, typed dates
and the threshold flag; no parties, contact fields, free text, attachment URLs
or raw bodies. Original document indexes survive filtering. Source policy
requires the exact public FTS endpoint, reviewed organizational scope and page
limit 100. Paging cannot change origin, path or query filters. Failures retain
fixed reasons without response bodies. Nothing configures this source in the
installed service or makes it the primary source.

The rule accepts separately reviewed scope rather than guessing it from PA
labels, signature dates, missing light-touch flags or threshold values. The
caller must cite the applicable category and exclusions. The ordinary profile
in the missing-entry packets is a candidate template, not a finding that the
ordinary category applies; its placeholder evaluation period is explicitly
labelled as unobserved. No statutory margin is defined for these missing-entry
records. Before-commencement and explicitly excluded constructed cases produce
NOT_APPLICABLE once entry is established. Public-access dates can establish a
timely upper bound with admitted entry/scope. A late public date remains
UNASSESSABLE unless a separate cited review establishes the earliest qualifying
publication event, including acknowledgement/alternative routes. There is no
signature-based one-sided inference.

## Hand-checkable arithmetic

Real notice `107781-2026`, contract `1`, publishes signature date November 3
and notice publication November 12 at 18:15:10Z. Its recorded signature date
is treated as a civil date, not as evidence of an actual midnight event.
Publication is converted to the Europe/London civil date for this **plain
measurement only**:

```text
signature-to-publication gap = November 12 - November 3 = 9 calendar days
legal entry date = not admitted
statutory margin = undefined (null)
legal result = UNASSESSABLE
```

Across all 150 rows this measurement ranges from 0 to 447 days, with zero
negative gaps. Among the 45 above-threshold-flagged rows it ranges from 0 to
349. These gaps are not legal lateness, bounds on contract entry, or a ranking
of suspected conduct.

A **constructed, explicitly synthetic** ordinary-contract fixture separately
proves HOLDS with the same care as BREAKS. In that fixture alone, an admitted
entry source states November 3 and an admitted publication source states
November 12; reviewed scope supplies the ordinary category:

```text
elapsed calendar days = November 12 - November 3 = 9
inclusive statutory day count = 9 + 1 = 10
profile limit = 30 days, section 53(1)(b)
margin = 30 - 10 = +20 days
result = HOLDS
last permitted civil date = December 2 (entry + 29 days)
```

The synthetic example does not add entry evidence to the real notice. Day 30
holds at margin zero. A confirmed earliest qualifying publication on day 31
breaks at margin -1; a merely displayed public notice on day 31 remains
UNASSESSABLE when earlier qualifying events are unresolved. The light-touch
fixture meets its distinct cited 120-day boundary. No arithmetic estimates
payments or harm.

## Adversarial self-review and changes

- **False hold from a signature proxy:** require an explicit typed entry event;
  refuse signature input. All live records consequently remain UNASSESSABLE.
- **False break from late public display:** require separate earliest-qualifying
  event evidence for BREAKS; otherwise the statutory margin stays null.
- **Wrong duty/category:** keep below-threshold and light-touch scope separate;
  no ordinary default from a missing flag. Welsh-regulation classifications in
  the initial candidate prevent treating it as a simple English reg-32 example.
- **Wrong event identity:** count only the contract-details document matching
  this release's notice ID. Preserve array indexes for exact source pointers.
- **Off-by-one or daylight-saving error:** count calendar dates inclusively,
  never elapsed 24-hour periods; test leap day, year boundary and DST season.
  Calendar-basis mismatch refuses assessment.
- **False completeness after network failure:** preserve transport, HTTP and
  malformed-page failures as incomplete runs, without raw error text; added
  three failure-receipt tests after review.
- **Evidence overwritten on rerun:** research capture scripts now refuse an
  existing output; a new capture needs a new output directory.
- **Legal assessability inflated by field coverage:** report both denominators
  and explicitly identify the missing source rather than inventing a date.

The strongest remaining objection is that the only HOLDS/BREAKS proofs are
synthetic: no admitted real entry/scope pair exists in this sample. That is
stated as a limit, not concealed by the 100% date-pair coverage. PR #18 stays
draft; this work does not close its first-assessable-real-rule gate.
