# Institutional watch and readable review pass — 19 October 2026

The preceding [research brief](INSTITUTIONAL_REBUILD_RESEARCH.md) reviewed official
OCDS, IATI, UN Comtrade, OCHA HAPI, World Bank Data360, BODS, SDMX, PROV and WCAG
materials, plus implementation references. Eight repositories were pinned and
14 primary artifacts recorded with hashes in the research workspace. Direct
institutional-site/API access is restricted in the current cloud environment;
this was documented rather than bypassed. Source documentation is not evidence
of live endpoint access, source completeness or institutional authorization.

## Implemented in this pass

- Global watch is the default entry. A shared country scope filters Records &
  review and carries questions into Investigate. Selected connections carry
  endpoint countries, reference year and evidence layer. Desks remain mounted
  after opening, preserving drafts and camera context; the hidden watch stops
  automatic rotation. Saved inquiry snapshots retain their own recorded scope.
- Fine moving chevrons replace glowing particle heads. Teal goods, pale material
  subset, indigo arms and amber supplied links have explicit names and direction.
  Animation communicates direction only. Coverage & source periods lists actual
  loaded evidence and unconnected domains. No additional global source feed is
  claimed by this rendering change.
- Guided delivery/payment forms submit exact decimal-string measurements,
  document references and revision identities. Blank observations remain unknown,
  an explicit 0 remains valid, and entered times disclose the browser timezone.
  Structured JSON intake remains under advanced integration.
- Operational cases, saved investigations and selected connections open readable
  HTML briefs with print/save-as-PDF controls. Reports retain source periods,
  exact comparisons, source links, review history and interpretation limits.
  Machine evidence packets remain advanced audit exports. Input/model text is
  escaped and unsafe source URL schemes are excluded in report rendering.
- Optional model assistance has bounded read-only tools for selected-evidence
  search, selected PDF pages, a scope-matching operational case and source-specific
  retained-scope statistics. Tool results actually sent to the model are added to
  the dossier and citation allowlist. Source/domain/period restrictions remain
  enforced independently of model instructions. No action/payment or arbitrary
  SQL tool is provided. Operations statistics scan latest 500 cases only.
- `SUNLIGHT_RESEARCH_API_KEY` is supported alongside the existing
  `OPENAI_API_KEY`. A saved managed-cloud draft adds the former secret requirement,
  the model variable and `api.openai.com` destination. It still requires review,
  save and publication in environment settings; it does not configure Render.

preview and supplied operational evidence. This pass does not connect worldwide
aid/debt/treasury/private-payment feeds, verify a global theft amount, add
institutional identities or exercise financial holds. The common typed global

research brief. Persistent Render storage and the remote rollout were not
verified from this environment. Existing source-connection, authentication and
storage requirements continue to apply.

## Persistent world and flow-tracking revision

The world is now the permanent surface, superseding the earlier separate-page
presentation and the hidden-watch rotation behaviour described above. Delivery,
research and records use independent surrounding docks, preserving the map's
camera and selection. Narrow screens retain the globe above the scrolling tool.
The connection register is a source-searching flow-tracking dock with readable
cards, source periods, explicit FROM/TO endpoints and a selected path. A selected
connection can carry into investigation without replacing the globe.

Canvas 2D now renders the land, borders, grid, reference points, conflict points,
routes and directional marks with one camera/clock. Automatic rotation does not
rebuild React/SVG geometry. Focus eases across the shorter longitude direction;
manual drag, country search and picking use the current camera. Repeated drawn
relationships share corridors while their individual source records remain
inspectable. Different bases, products or categories are not added as one value.
Dense contract-count balloons no longer cover the geography; figures remain in
their country/source controls.
