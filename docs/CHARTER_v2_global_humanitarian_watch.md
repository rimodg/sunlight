## 1. The principle

Every category of public value that moves across the world has four layers. A norm: the law, treaty or standard that says what may be done and what must be recorded. A record: the documents the norm obliges someone to create and keep. A trace: the identity that outlives the paperwork, a serial number, a lot, a plot's coordinates, a contract identifier, a certificate number, a count of people at an administrative level. An action chain: the body that can act when something is wrong. Corruption, diversion and neglect live in the gaps between those layers: a record never created, a document whose issuer had no authority, a trace whose chain ends somewhere the documents do not, a need documented and never served, a norm with no reader.

SUNLIGHT reads whether the documents, the records and the trace agree, cites the provision that requires each piece, and draws the missing one. It reads flows beside the documented need they were meant to serve and the documented harm the recording institutions themselves attributed. It never judges intent, never assesses whether a decision should have been made, never estimates harm, never asserts causation, and never publishes a finding before the institution whose norm it is has seen it. It is an inspector, not a judge, and the person who loses is a node in its graph, not a metaphor in its prose.

## 2. What SUNLIGHT becomes

**From one category to the watch.** Built for public procurement and validated there, SUNLIGHT proved this month the claim it had only asserted: a new category is a profile (thresholds, citations, category mappings, aliases, dated sanction tables), an adapter (the category's documents into the category's dossier shape), and a rule family (each rule citing its provision), with the core untouched and the floor byte-identical after. Run one, the procurement engine on forty arms-transfer records, found nothing; run two, the arms profile and rules on the same records, read the case as the treaty parties had filed it. The watch is that pattern repeated across the flows that shape human lives: procurement, arms, commodities out of poor countries, money in and out, aid in, and the human record beside all of them.

**Two positions, one instrument.** Inside an institution, SUNLIGHT is the passport at the gate: it reads the private chain (licences, certificates, contracts, disbursement files, delivery records) before value moves and hands the institution a finding it can act on. Outside, it reads the public shadow of that chain (annual reports, registers, customs mirrors, open contracting data, panel reports, field tracing, needs plans, funding trackers) and produces the same readings, reproducibly, in the Landmine Monitor tradition. Same rules, same citations, same refusals. What differs is which nodes the data supplies, and the fidelity map states that per run.

**The human layer as the join.** Flows are read beside the documented need and the documented consequence: a humanitarian plan's requirement against its funding, a district's food-security phase against the deliveries contracted for it, a state's declared and undeclared resource outflows placed beside its own budget statements and its own humanitarian requirement for the same year, arms declared into a destination placed beside the casualty recorder's counts and the recorder's attribution. The instrument draws no line between them; it puts them in one graph with their sources, which nothing else does.

**The Registry as the join of joins.** The shared nodes are where the categories meet: a hub that is a gold destination, an arms recipient and a contractor base; a transit state in both the gold and the arms chains; a country that is a resource origin, an embargoed destination and the site of a famine. The Registry persists resolved parties, hubs, brokers, transporters, refiners, contractors, and places with their documented populations, across category profiles, with corpus stamps, so that a node's roles across flows are read as one structure.

**The organs, generalised.** Side 1 becomes chain verification before release, for any instrument. Side 2 becomes delivery verified against release. Side 3 becomes cross-record pattern reading with a profile-supplied vocabulary. Side 4 and the Absence Ledger become the Need Ledger: the remediation half of any norm, what was recovered, where it went, what the absence left unserved, in the institution's own documents. Side 5 becomes corroboration against evidence that does not pass through the interested party, with the physical trace as a seventh class. Side 7, designed, becomes the routing registry per category. The Registry, designed, becomes the memory of the watch.

## 3. What it means for the world, without inflation

The anti-corruption norm has measurement in the millions and investigation in the hundreds and nothing between them at the level of the contract. The arms-trade norm has a reporting obligation and no reader; its own civil-society monitor matches under two percent of reported exports to imports by hand. Between 321 and 474 tonnes of African gold leave the continent undeclared every year, at least 435 tonnes in 2022 worth about 31 billion dollars, found by the same mirror reading SUNLIGHT now performs deterministically. The UN's own procurement engine lent sixty-three million dollars to a borrower with no capability to deliver, and a blogger found it. In 2025 the humanitarian system asked for 47 billion dollars and received about 12, reached 25 million fewer people than the year before, confirmed famine in two places at once for the first time in its history, and lost comparable data for eighteen countries, Burkina Faso among them. Every one of those facts sits in a record that nobody reads structurally, and none of the records are read together.

A system that reads all of them, deterministically, from documents anyone can download, with every finding traceable to a provision, and keeps the documented human need in the same graph as the flows, changes four things. The weakest states get the strictest bar rather than the weakest, because calibration comes from the intersection of mature systems rather than from local capacity. Findings travel into the institutions' own action chains instead of dying in reports. An integrity measurement exists that is not a perception survey, decomposable to rules and citations, reproducible anywhere. And the gap between what was needed, what was sent, what arrived and what it left unmet becomes a ledger rather than a press release.

What it does not mean. SUNLIGHT discovers no new facts; it reads what was filed. It does not enforce; it routes. It does not decide whether a transfer was responsible, a contract corrupt, or a famine caused; people do. It counts no one the institutions did not count, and where they did not count, it says so. Its readings are only as good as the fidelity of what it was given, and every packet states that fidelity. A watch that oversold itself would be worse than none in every category, and worst in the one where lives are the unit.

## 4. The constitution and the disciplines

**The eight lines, unchanged and never moving.** Determinism, no model in the analytical path. Every output a risk indicator, never an allegation. The DOJ recall floor absolute and re-read live after any core-touching change. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, enforced in five places. The core touched only on proof of computation defect. None is not zero. Framing gates at every layer. No em-dashes anywhere.

**Four domain lines, added on the same footing.** The instrument never assesses ATT Article 7 or its equivalents in any category; whether a decision should have been made is human. It never names a state, vendor, hub or person as having violated anything; it reads records. It never estimates harm; consequences are stated only in the institution's own documented terms, or "not stated." It never asserts causation; where a recording institution attributes a driver or a cause, the instrument quotes the attribution with its source and adds nothing.

**Two lines the runs taught us.** Zero filings are not zero transfers: a baseline that reads zero because no report was filed is UNASSESSABLE, not a history of nothing, enforced in code with a coverage gate. And juxtaposition is not arithmetic: two documented facts may be placed side by side with their sources; the instrument performs no operation between them.

**The protection line.** The human layer is aggregate only, at the administrative levels the institutions themselves publish. The instrument holds no personal data, touches no beneficiary registry or biometric system, and never publishes a location of a vulnerable population beyond what the recording institution has published. A disclosure state stricter than internal-only, protection-sensitive, exists for any reading involving a live conflict, and a do-no-harm review precedes any such observation.

**The four building disciplines, each learned from a failure.** Ground truth before code. Uniqueness-asserted patches. Receipts before advancing. Block-and-verify, never chained.

**The review gate for any change.** Constitutional compliance, absolute; fit with how the institution actually works; coverage of typologies the institution itself documented; reduction of adoption friction; strategic position. Proposals that would soften a truthfulness property to solve an operational problem are refused and their concern routed elsewhere; two were, this month.

## 6. The encoding

### 6.1 Dossier shapes

ContractDossier (IN TREE, 38 fields). TransferRecord (IN TREE, arms). To add: TransferChain and DisbursementChain (typed graphs of one transfer's or disbursement's documents and items), CommodityFlowRecord (reporter, partner, chapter, year, quantity, value, declared side, source), NeedRecord (place at administrative level, year, plan, cluster, people in need, targeted, requirement, funded, phase, stated driver, source), ItemIdentity (make, model, calibre, serial; lot; plot polygon; certificate number), Party (resolved entity with aliases, roles, jurisdictions), Place (administrative unit with documented population). Every dossier carries identifier, locator, content hash and an ingestion-quality report.

### 6.2 Chain schema and required-node tables

A chain is a typed graph. Each category profile carries a required-node table: node type, the provision requiring it, the responsible party, and whether absence is MISSING (required and absent), UNAVAILABLE (required but not readable from this position), or NOT APPLICABLE. The MISSING node is a finding with citation; the UNAVAILABLE node is a fidelity statement; nothing infers from either. The human layer's chain is need, requirement, funding, contract, delivery, corroboration.

### 6.3 Profile model

JurisdictionProfile (IN TREE) and ATTProfile (IN TREE); a common base to extract: name and version; per-rule citations keyed by rule id (never a whole dictionary on a fire); thresholds; category-mapping tables; alias tables; dated sanction and embargo tables with scope and exemptions; required-node tables; queryable evidence classes and reachability; delegation thresholds; the action-chain registry; the protection policy for the human layer. Profiles are versioned data referencing global parameter versions.

### 6.4 Rule contract

Every rule returns one shape: rule id, name, layer, severity, status (INDICATOR, UNASSESSABLE, UNAVAILABLE), subject keys, description, evidence, legal citation specific to the rule, sources. Pure functions over a dossier or ledger plus a profile plus a coverage map of what was filed. No wall clock. Sorted iteration. Total on degenerate input. Tested for determinism, totality, refusal wording on generated outputs, and threshold sourcing from the profile.

### 6.5 Evidence classes and the gate

Six classes plus the physical trace. Artifacts hashed with provenance. Four verdicts with UNVERIFIED distinct from CONTRADICTED. The gate generalises: no finding escalates without independent dimensions in agreement, named per category; the arms run showed why (history alone is a market-entry screen; with concentration, transit, non-filing and field tracing it describes a pipeline).

### 6.6 Patterns, routing, Registry, Need Ledger

Side 3's vocabulary becomes profile-supplied (the current procurement wording attributes motive and misreads other domains). Side 7 becomes an action-chain registry per category, recommendation only. The Registry persists parties, hubs, brokers, transporters, refiners, contractors and places with documented populations across categories, seeded by the Annual Statistical Report, the Register, Comtrade partners and the needs plans. The Need Ledger generalises the Absence Ledger: for a place and year, documented need, documented funding, contracted delivery, verified delivery, corroborated outcome, and what remains, in the plan's own units, with the same framing gates and the same refusal to estimate.

### 6.7 Adapters

IN TREE: OCDS, USAspending, IATI (reported), Quantum and Compass stubs. OBSERVED outside the tree: the Register compilation adapter (coordinate bands per page, split headers, categories persisting per state, 0.7 percent quarantined). To add with fixtures and tests: the Register adapter including import tables; ATT reports by manual download; COARM; Comtrade chapters 18, 71 and 93 with a key; national export reports; iTrace and panel reports; ASR and UNGM; EITI; FTS, IPC, needs plans and casualty recorders through HDX and their APIs. Every adapter emits sourced rows with locator and hash and a fidelity map.

### 6.8 API surface

IN TREE: /analyze, /batch, /alerts/triage, /delivery/batch, /evidence/batch, recovery and absence routes, /profiles, /version, /health. To add: /transfers/batch, /flows/batch, /chains/verify, /need/ledger, domain-profile listing, and AlertInput accepting domain profiles. Authentication at the institution's boundary.

### 6.9 Floors and reproducibility

The DOJ floor for procurement; per-category reference sets for the others (panel-documented diversions for arms; Swissaid's country figures as the external check for gold; adjudicated cases for UN disbursements; the GRFC and FTS as the external check for the human layer). Corpus stamps on every output; a conformance script for institutions; provenance attestation on releases.

### 6.10 Observation environments

A top-level observatory directory per category and country: sources with hashes and dates, adapters, fidelity maps, traces per record, a corrections log, disclosure states (internal_only_pending_institutional_channel; protection_sensitive). Read-only against the engine. Environment corrections free; rules on necessity with citation and a floor read; profile data on evidence; core only on proven computation defect.
